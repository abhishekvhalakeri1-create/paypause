The trap

The trap isn't an accident. It's the business model.

Signup is one big green button. Getting out is a maze of hidden menus, guilt trips and dead-end support channels — designed to run out the clock on day seven.

Cancellation buried on purpose

The subscribe button is giant and green. The cancel link lives four menus deep, behind a "Are you sure you want to lose everything?" guilt trip and a chatbot that pretends not to understand.

Renewal dates hidden in plain sight

No reminder. No calendar invite. Just a quiet charge at 2 a.m. on day eight — often at a higher "regular price" than the trial teaser you signed up for.

Your real card, on file forever

Every trial demands a real card number. One missed deadline and it's ₹499 a month until you notice on a statement you barely read. Your card details stay in their database long after you leave.

And the "protection" apps? They want the keys to your bank.

Most subscription trackers ask for your online banking username and password — the one credential you should never hand a third party. PayPause never does. We issue virtual cards instead, so your real banking details never touch us or the merchant.
